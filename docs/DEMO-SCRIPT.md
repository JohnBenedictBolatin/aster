# Aster three-minute demo script

## Primary walkthrough · recovery and fatigue

**0:00–0:20 · Orient**

Open **Home** and choose the recovery review. Say: “Aster is an offline demonstration of how a crew member could notice a change, inspect its evidence, and decide what to do next. The records are demo data; Aster is not a clinical tool.” Point out the locally saved indicator.

**0:20–0:55 · Understand the finding**

Read **What changed?** and **Your next step** in plain language. Say: “Sleep and resting pulse differ from this crew member’s recent pattern. That tells us to review the change; it doesn’t explain why.” Open **Why this suggestion?** to show the supporting records and the historical comparison details.

**0:55–1:25 · Inspect a reading**

Open **My readings**. Show the value, time, collection method, conditions, and history. Explain that the comparison uses the crew member’s own matching history and is not a clinical range. Point out that missing, stale, or questionable readings remain visible and are not shown as normal.

**1:25–2:05 · Choose an action**

Return to **Health reviews** and open the recovery review. Follow its suggested action, or open **Other actions** to show that a check-in and the 60-second reaction demonstration are optional. Say: “The reaction check adds context. It is not NASA’s research instrument or an operational readiness test.” Schedule a follow-up to show the editable time and saved task.

**2:05–2:35 · Reassess with new evidence**

Open **Presenter controls** and choose **Values move toward personal history**. Return to the review and open the follow-up result. Point out that the new values are a separate recorded outcome, the task needs an explicit review, and the investigation does not close automatically.

**2:35–3:00 · Preserve what remains unknown**

Choose **Prepare review summary** and save the draft locally. Say: “This records the evidence and action for a later review. The demo does not send a real message or promise a response.”

## Alternate walkthroughs

### Cabin-environment concern

Open **Presenter controls** and switch to **Cabin environment**. In **Health reviews**, open the cabin review and expand **Check the instrument** to inspect source, time, and signal quality. Open **Why this suggestion?** to see the overlapping sensor and symptom records. Explain that timing helps organize a review but does not prove a cause. Use Presenter controls to mark sensor contact uncertain; show that the new record requires verification.

### Radiation-context review

Switch to **Radiation context** in Presenter controls and open its health review. Expand **Check the instrument** and inspect the source, original units, timestamps, and visible data gap. In the radiation records, distinguish the fictional mission readings from the historical NASA RadLab ISS/DosTel excerpt. Expand **Related radiation-monitoring research** to show that CSA’s CANS work is a separate instrument-development reference. Say: “These records do not give this crew member a personal dose, and a data gap stays unknown.”

## Reset and rehearsal

Use **Presenter controls → Reset this investigation** to restore that scenario’s demonstration fixtures; live heart-rate records remain separate. Practice the primary path once before presenting. The browser preview works offline after its assets load. The Windows installer has been generated but not install/reopen tested; present the browser preview unless you have personally verified the installed build.
